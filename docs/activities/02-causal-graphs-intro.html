2  Introduction to Causal Graphs

Goals

  • Motivate why causal graphs are useful in causal inference investigations
  • Practice graphically showing our beliefs about data-generating processes using causal graphs

Small Group Discussion

Prompt 1

We are interested in estimating the causal effect of owning a dog on longevity.

Do you think it’s important to use the following variables in our analysis?

  • Income
  • General physical activity
  • Change in physical activity after getting a dog

First, think through this without using a causal diagram. Next, draw a causal diagram with “dog ownership”, “longevity”, and the above variables. How did the two approaches (using vs. not using a causal diagram) compare (in terms of ease or other factors)?

Practice

Research question: Should organizations implement a paid parental leave policy to improve employee satisfaction?

Most likely, only cross-sectional data would be available via company websites (for treatment information and company characteristics) and websites like Glassdoor (for outcome data).

  1. Individually, open DAGitty, and spend about 5 minutes drawing a causal graph for this data context.
    • Add the treatment and outcome variables and label them as such.
    • Add other relevant variables to the diagram.
    • Add edges depicting direct causal relationships between variables.
    • Indicate which variables would likely be unobservable.
  2. As a group, compare your diagrams.
    • Where do your diagrams agree?
    • Where do they disagree? Are these differences resolvable?
  3. Reflect on this process as a group.
    • How did the process feel?
    • What was challenging or confusing?
    • Is there anything you’re wondering about?
